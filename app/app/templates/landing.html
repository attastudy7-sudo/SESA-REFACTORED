The core loop
From search to mastery in one session
No tab-switching. No distractions. No forgetting everything a week later.
01
Search any topic

Type in a subject — "Calculus 1," "Organic Chemistry," "Data Structures" — and Knowly surfaces curated videos from verified, high-quality YouTube educators. No entertainment content. No algorithm rabbit holes. Just the best academic material on that exact topic, ranked and filtered for you.

No account needed to browse
02
Enter the Study Room

Click any video and you're in — a distraction-free Study Room built around that content. Watch the video with AI-generated chapter markers so you can jump to exactly what you need. A Smart Timeline shows you key concepts as they appear in the video in real time. Creator credit stays front and centre — you're always one click away from subscribing to the original channel.

Guests can watch fully — no wall
03
Generate your Study Pack

When you're ready, hit generate. In seconds, Knowly's AI builds a complete Study Pack: structured notes from the video transcript, a formula cheatsheet, a flashcard deck with every key term, and a quiz to test what actually stuck. Study Packs are stored permanently to your account — come back before exams and the same videos, same notes, same quiz are right where you left them.

Free users get 2 generations per day
04
Work through the pack

Your Study Pack is structured as a learning path — not a pile of content. Each video unlocks a 3-question micro-quiz. Pass it, move to the next video. Struggle with a question? The AI shows you exactly where in the notes it was covered and offers an alternate explanation using a different analogy. Finish all videos and pass the Final Boss Quiz to earn your Mastery Badge.

Spaced repetition built in
05
Track what needs work

Your Dashboard tracks every topic you've studied and flags your weakest areas automatically. Concepts you repeatedly miss become priority flashcards in your daily review deck. Your Aura score — Knowly's reputation system — reflects genuine mastery, not just activity. The leaderboard rewards students who actually understand the material.

Weak topics automatically surface
Inside a Study Pack
Everything in one place, nothing missing
Every Study Pack contains six types of content, automatically generated and linked together.
Structured Notes

AI reads the full video transcript and organises it into headed sections, bullet points, and callout boxes. Your notes, your AI, no copy-paste.

Formula Cheatsheet

Every formula, definition, and key term extracted and rendered in clean LaTeX. Perfect for STEM topics. One page covers the whole video.

Flashcard Deck

Atomic Q&A cards, one concept per card, tagged to the exact video timestamp they came from. Review mode, shuffle mode, or grind the ones you keep missing.

Quiz Engine

Micro-quizzes (3 questions) after each video to lock in understanding before you move on, plus a comprehensive Final Boss Quiz at the end of the full pack.

Curated Video Sequence

Not just one video — 3 to 5 videos on the same topic, sequenced from foundation to deep dive. The system picks the best match from verified educators on that exact concept.

AI Tutor Chat

Highlight any line in your notes and ask the AI to simplify it, expand on it, or explain it using an analogy. The AI is grounded in your specific pack content — no hallucinations.

Remix & Share

Clone any public Study Pack to your account and customise it. Add your own notes, swap a video, adjust the quiz. Your remixed version stays in your vault forever.

Weak Topic Alerts

Topics you fail in quizzes get flagged as Critical in your dashboard. The AI surfaces them in your next session automatically — before you forget you forgot them.

The Study Room
A full learning environment, not just a video player
When you open a Study Pack, you enter the Study Room — a focused workspace with everything accessible in one view, nothing to pull you away.
Smart Progress Bar

A persistent tracker fills as you complete each video, quiz, and flashcard set. See exactly how close you are to pack mastery at a glance.

Dynamic Syllabus

A side nav checklist that unlocks each item — quiz, next video, flashcards — only after the previous step is done. Keeps your focus linear and your brain in flow state.

Chapter Anchors

Long videos are automatically segmented into clickable chapters by the AI. Jump to "Integration by Parts starts here" without scrubbing through an hour of content.

Library Ambience

See how many other students are studying the same pack right now — anonymously. The social pressure of a virtual library without the distractions of a real one.

Highlight & Ask

Select any text in your notes to expand, simplify, or convert to a flashcard instantly. Deep-dives on exactly the part that confused you, not a re-read of the whole topic.

Focus Guard

Detects when you've paused or drifted for too long and nudges you back in. Optionally suggests a 5-minute Pomodoro break so you return sharper, not exhausted.

FAQ
Common questions
Do I need to sign up to watch videos?
No. You can search topics and watch any video in the Study Room without an account. Generating Study Packs, saving progress, and accessing the AI Tutor requires a free account.
Are the AI-generated notes accurate?
Notes are generated from the video transcript and are as accurate as what the educator says. For STEM content, formulas are validated and rendered in LaTeX. We recommend using the notes alongside the video, not as a replacement for it. If you spot an issue, you can edit your notes directly in the pack.
Are there ads on Knowly?
Knowly itself is ad-free. Videos are played via YouTube's official embed which may include YouTube's own ads — we have no control over these and removing them would violate YouTube's terms. We display clear creator credit under every video so you can support the original channel directly.
How does Aura differ from XP?
Aura is the social-facing reputation score visible on your profile and the leaderboard. It reflects genuine study activity — packs completed, quizzes passed, packs shared. Think of it as your academic credibility on the platform.
Can I use Knowly for any subject?
Knowly currently covers 50+ university-level subjects with a focus on Ghanaian programmes. The library is growing weekly. If your subject isn't well covered yet, you can upload your own notes or PDFs and Knowly will generate a Study Pack around your material plus matching videos from our curated library.
What happens to my Study Packs if I cancel Pro?
All your packs stay in your account on the Free plan. You keep everything you've generated. You just return to the Free quota limit for new generations.
Is my data private?
Your private packs are only visible to you. Public packs and your Aura score appear on your profile. We don't sell your data and we don't run ads. Your quiz scores and weak topic data are used only to personalise your study experience.
