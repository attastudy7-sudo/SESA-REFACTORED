Why We Exist - The Silent Crisis in Ghana's Classrooms

Across Ghana, students navigate anxiety, depression, and social isolation largely unseen. Traditional academic assessments measure what students know — but not how they feel.

Without data on emotional wellbeing, schools cannot intervene early, and students silently disengage.

Our Story - From Research to Action

FOPA Dilligent Consult was born from a simple observation: Ghanaian schools have the infrastructure to teach, but not the tools to listen.

Our founder, Dr. Felix Oppusu Paapa Agyiri, spent years researching curriculum alignment and student support services before recognising that emotional wellbeing was the missing piece.

What began as academic research — surveying students in the Kwahu East District — evolved into SESA: a validated assessment instrument designed specifically for the Ghanaian school context.

“We don’t just assess students. We give schools the language to understand what their students are feeling.”

Dr. Felix Oppusu Paapa Agyiri, Founder

How It Works - Our Approach

SESA is not a one-size-fits-all tool. It's a structured, culturally grounded process designed for the Ghanaian classroom.

  • 01 Assess. Students complete a validated questionnaire measuring emotional wellbeing across six dimensions — in under 20 minutes.
  • 02 Analyse. Results are scored and contextualised against GES benchmarks, giving schools a clear picture of where students stand.
  • 03 Recommend. Schools receive targeted action plans — specific, practical steps to improve emotional support where it's needed most.
  • 04 Support. FOPA provides ongoing consultation to help schools implement changes, train staff, and track progress over time.
