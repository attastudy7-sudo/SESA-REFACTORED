What we measure - The 6 Dimensions of Emotional Safety

Our audit evaluates the ecosystem of the child across these critical pillars, using clinical indicators to map their emotional landscape.

06

Dimension 01 / Validated Instrument

Cognitive Resilience

Evaluating the ability to manage stress and bounce back from academic setbacks and life challenges.

Dimension 02 / Validated Instrument

Relational Trust

Measuring the quality of peer-to-peer and student-teacher connections within the school ecosystem.

Dimension 03 / Validated Instrument

Emotional Autonomy

Assessing the student's capacity for independent emotional regulation and self-awareness.

Dimension 04 / Validated Instrument

Sense of Belonging

Understanding the student's perception of value and integration within the school culture.

Dimension 05 / Validated Instrument

Behavioural Safety

Screening for environments free from bullying, social exclusion, and physical threats.

Dimension 06 / Validated Instrument

Future Orientation

Identifying long-term motivation, positive academic aspirations, and future goal setting.

Result Protocol - The Traffic Light System

Our staged results system ensures clarity in intervention. Every assessment is categorised into one of four distinct stages, guiding educators and psychologists on the next steps.

N

Normal

Optimal emotional health. The child demonstrates resilience and strong supportive networks.

M

Mild

Transient stressors identified. Recommend standard pastoral care monitoring and routine check-ins.

E

Elevated

Significant emotional distress markers. Tier-2 intervention required: targeted counselling sessions.

C

Clinical

Acute psychological distress. Immediate clinical referral and comprehensive parental intervention.
