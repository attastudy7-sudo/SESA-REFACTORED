Register your school in about two minutes with the setup wizard.

School Registration

How to register your school on the SESA platform. Registering takes about two minutes — the form walks you through four short steps.

  • Step 1: Your School. Enter your school’s full official name. This name must be unique across the SESA platform, so if another school has already registered with the same name, you will be asked to choose a different one. The name you enter will be visible to students when they join.
  • Step 2: Administrator. Provide the administrator’s full name, and optionally an email address and phone number. Email and phone are not required, but providing them makes it easier for our team to reach you about your account.
  • Step 3: Set a Password. Choose a password with at least 8 characters. You will use this password along with your administrator name to sign in to your school dashboard. There is no email-based password recovery — if you forget your password, contact FOPA Dilligent Consult directly.
  • Step 4: Confirm & Register. Review your details on the summary card. Tick the data processing consent checkbox, which confirms you are authorised to register the school and agree to the collection and processing of school and student data in accordance with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Click Register School to complete.

What happens next. There is no email verification step. Once you register, you are automatically signed in and taken straight to your school dashboard. You can immediately upload students, create classes, and explore the full dashboard — everything except student onboarding is free. A subscription is only required to generate access codes and give students access to their dashboards.

Account Activation

Uploading students and exploring your dashboard is free. A subscription is only needed to bring your students online — the student registration link, QR code, claim codes, and student dashboards unlock after payment.

How to activate. On your dashboard, you will see a subscription prompt with a Subscribe button. Click it to open the Paystack payment popup. The subscription costs GHS 100 per year. Complete the payment using any of the supported methods (card, mobile money, bank transfer). Once Paystack confirms your payment, student access is activated automatically — there is no manual approval step.

Subscription details. Your subscription lasts 365 days from the date of payment. You will see a reminder on your dashboard when your subscription is approaching expiry. If your subscription expires, students will not be able to log in until it is renewed — your uploaded data, dashboard, and analytics remain fully available to you.

Bulk Student Upload

Bulk upload is the fastest way to add your entire student body at once. SESA creates accounts for every student automatically.

Requirements.

  • No payment needed — upload is free for every registered school.
  • An Excel file in .xlsx or .xls format. CSV files are not accepted.
  • Maximum file size: 5 MB. Maximum 1,000 students per upload — split larger bodies into multiple files.
  • Use only the official SESA template. Do not rename, add, or remove columns. Delete the 5 sample rows before uploading your own data.

What happens after upload. Each student receives an auto-generated username ({first initial}{lastname}, e.g. amamensah, with a number appended on duplicates), a temporary password (their student_id, or username if blank), and a 6-character claim code. Uploaded students are not yet claimed — they must activate their accounts before they can log in.

Upload Column Reference

The upload template has columns in row 4. Only two are required; everything else is optional but recommended — the more data you provide, the richer your dashboard reports will be.

How usernames are generated. Usernames are created automatically in the format {first initial}{last name}, all lowercase, with non-alphanumeric characters removed — Ama Mensah becomes amamensah, then amamensah1, amamensah2 on duplicates. Note: this differs from join-by-code, which produces firstname.lastname.schoolcode. Both formats work for logging in.

Step-by-Step Upload

Follow these steps to upload your student file.

  • 1. Download and open the template. Open it in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets. Student data starts at row 5: rows 1 through 3 contain instructions, and row 4 contains the column headers.
  • 2. Delete the sample rows. The template includes 5 sample student rows. Delete all of them before entering your own data. Do not modify the headers in row 4.
  • 3. Fill in student data. Enter one student per row. Only first_name and last_name are required. If you want students to log in with a specific password, set their student_id to that password.
  • 4. Save as Excel. Save the file in .xlsx or .xls format — CSV files are not accepted. In Google Sheets, go to File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).
  • 5. Upload on your dashboard. Go to the Upload Students section, click Choose File, select your Excel file, then click Upload. The file must be under 5 MB with no more than 1,000 rows.
  • 6. Check the result. You will see how many accounts were created and how many rows were skipped (blank names or duplicates). Then print the claim codes so students can activate their accounts.

Students Join by Code

If you prefer not to use Excel upload, students can join your school individually using a join code.

Each school has a unique 6-character access code (e.g. A3F9B2) and a corresponding QR code. On your dashboard, find the access code section in the right sidebar — if you haven’t generated a code yet, click Generate Code. You can regenerate a new code at any time; the old one stops working immediately.

Share your access code with the student, or direct them to scan your QR code. The student goes to the SESA homepage and clicks Join with Code, enters the 6-character code, confirms your school name, then fills in first name, last name, and a password (minimum 6 characters). Their account is created and they are signed in immediately.

From your dashboard you can open a printable QR flyer — print it and display it on your school notice board. Use bulk upload for onboarding many students at once; use join by code for mid-term enrolments or schools that prefer self-registration.

Claim Codes

When you bulk-upload students, their accounts are created but not yet activated. Each student receives a unique 6-character claim code.

Printing claim codes. After a successful upload, click Print Claim Codes on your dashboard. Each slip shows the student’s name and class, their auto-generated username, their 6-character claim code, and the claim URL. Cut the slips and distribute them — students should keep their claim code private.

How students claim. Go to the SESA homepage and click Sign In, then Activate Account (or go directly to /claim). Enter the 6-character claim code, set a new password (minimum 6 characters), and click Activate Account.

Important details. Students cannot log in until they claim their account. Claim codes are case-sensitive and each code can only be used once. If a student loses their code, re-print claim codes from the dashboard.

Password Reset

If a student forgets their password, they can reset it themselves — with no email required. The reset uses the school’s access code as verification.

How to reset. Go to the SESA login page and click Forgot your password? Enter the school access code (the same 6-character code used for student join), the student’s username, and a new password (minimum 8 characters). The student can now log in with the new password.

What the student needs. Their school’s access code and their username (find it on the dashboard student list if unknown). Treat the access code as confidential and regenerate it periodically — anyone who knows it plus a username can reset that password.

Troubleshooting

Solutions to the most common upload, login, and claim-code issues.

  • “Your school’s subscription is not active yet.” Student registration and logins are locked until the school subscribes. An administrator should complete payment via Paystack on the dashboard — student access unlocks instantly on confirmation.
  • “Missing required columns.” Headers in row 4 must read first_name and last_name exactly — all lowercase, underscores, no spaces. Do not modify the template headers.
  • “Invalid file type.” Only .xlsx and .xls files are accepted. From Google Sheets, download as Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) before uploading.
  • Some rows were skipped. Rows are skipped when first_name or last_name is blank, or when the name already exists in your school. Check for empty rows or duplicates and re-upload.
  • Level or gender not showing correctly. These fields only accept exact lowercase values — jhs, shs, or university; male, female, or other. Capitalised versions are ignored.
  • Account locked after too many attempts. After 5 failed logins the account locks for 15 minutes. Wait and try again, or verify the username and password.
  • Student can’t log in (redirected to claim page). The account has not been claimed yet — the student must enter their claim code and set a password first.
  • Claim code not working. Codes are case-sensitive and single-use. Re-print claim codes from the dashboard for updated slips.
  • Students can’t find the login page. Direct them to the SESA homepage to click Sign In, selecting the regular login — not School Administrator Login.
