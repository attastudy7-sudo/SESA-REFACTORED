School Registration

How to register your school on the SESA platform.

{# ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════ SECTIONS — article content, one visible at a time ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════ #} {# ── Registration ── #}

Registering your school on SESA takes about two minutes. On the SESA homepage, click Register My School to open the registration wizard. The form walks you through four short steps.

Step 1 — Your School

Enter your school's full official name. This name must be unique across the SESA platform — if another school has already registered with the same name, you will be asked to choose a different one. The name you enter will be visible to students when they join.

Step 2 — Administrator

Provide the administrator's full name, and optionally an email address and phone number. Email and phone are not required, but providing them makes it easier for our team to reach you about your account.

Step 3 — Set a Password

Choose a password with at least 8 characters. You will use this password along with your administrator name to sign in to your school dashboard. There is no email-based password recovery — if you forget your password, contact FOPA DILLIGENT CONSULT directly.

Step 4 — Confirm & Register

Review your details on the summary card. Tick the data processing consent checkbox — this confirms you are authorised to register the school and agree to the collection and processing of school and student data in accordance with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Click Register School to complete.

What happens next

There is no email verification step. Once you register, you are automatically signed in and taken straight to your school dashboard. However, student upload and other advanced features will not be available until your subscription is activated — see Account Activation for the next step.

{# ── Activation ── #}

After registering, your dashboard will show a limited view. Student upload, access codes, and other advanced features are locked until your subscription is activated through payment.

How to activate

On your dashboard, you will see a subscription prompt with a Subscribe button. Click it to open the Paystack payment popup. The subscription costs GHS 100 per year. Complete the payment using any of the supported methods (card, mobile money, bank transfer).

Once Paystack confirms your payment, your account is activated automatically — there is no manual approval step. Upload access is enabled immediately, and you can start adding students right away.

Subscription details

  • Your subscription lasts 365 days from the date of payment.
  • You will see a reminder on your dashboard when your subscription is approaching expiry.
  • If your subscription expires, student upload will be disabled again. You can renew at any time from the dashboard.
  • Students who already have accounts can continue to log in even when your subscription expires — only new uploads are affected.
If you experience any issues with payment, or need to arrange a different payment method, contact FOPA DILLIGENT CONSULT directly.
{# ── First Login ── #}

To access your school portal, go to the SESA homepage and click Sign In. On the login page, select School Administrator Login and enter the administrator name and password you chose during registration.

Account security

Your account is protected by a lockout mechanism. After 5 failed login attempts, your account will be locked for 15 minutes. During this time, you cannot sign in even with the correct password. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or contact FOPA DILLIGENT CONSULT if the issue persists.

Your dashboard at a glance

Once signed in, you will see your school dashboard with the following sections:

  • Monthly wellness trends — a line chart showing student assessment scores over time.
  • Vital statistics — total students, total assessments, and your school's overall wellness score.
  • Student list — a searchable list of all registered students with their status (Normal, Mild, Elevated, At Risk).
  • Upload Students — the section where you upload your Excel file to bulk-add students.
  • Access code & QR code — your school's unique join code and QR flyer for student self-registration.

If your subscription is not yet active, the upload section will show a prompt to subscribe. You can still view your dashboard overview, but student data will be empty until you add students.

{# ── Upload ── #}

Bulk upload is the fastest way to add your entire student body at once. You upload an Excel file with your student data, and SESA creates accounts for every student automatically.

Requirements

  • An active subscription — upload is locked until payment is confirmed.
  • An Excel file in .xlsx or .xls format. CSV files are not accepted.
  • Maximum file size: 5 MB.
  • Maximum 1,000 students per upload. If you have more students, split them into multiple files and upload separately.

Download the template

Use only the official SESA template. Do not rename, add, or remove columns — the uploader depends on the exact column headers. The template includes 5 sample rows that you must delete before uploading your own data.

What happens after upload

SESA creates an account for each valid row. You will see a success message showing how many accounts were created and how many rows were skipped. Each student receives:

  • An auto-generated username in the format {first initial}{lastname} (e.g. amamensah). If that username already exists in your school, a number is appended (e.g. amamensah1).
  • A temporary password — either their student_id from the Excel file, or their username if no student_id was provided.
  • A claim code — a 6-character code printed on claim slips that students use to activate their account and set their own password.

Uploaded students are not yet claimed. They must activate their accounts using their claim code before they can log in. See Claim Codes for details.

{# ── Columns ── #}

The upload template has columns in row 4. Only two are required — everything else is optional but recommended. The more data you provide, the richer your dashboard reports will be.

Column Required? Description Example
first_name Required Student's first name Ama
last_name Required Student's last name Mensah
student_id Optional School's student ID. Used as initial password. If blank, username is used instead. STU001
class_group Optional Class or form group 3A, Form 2
level Optional Must be exactly one of: jhs, shs, or university jhs
gender Optional Must be exactly one of: male, female, or other female
birthdate Optional Date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format 2008-04-12
email Optional Student email address. Duplicates across the system are saved without email. ama@school.edu

How usernames are generated

Usernames are created automatically in the format {first initial}{last name}, all lowercase, with non-alphanumeric characters removed. For example, Ama Mensah becomes amamensah. If that username already exists within your school, a number is added: amamensah1, amamensah2, and so on.

Note: this is different from the format used when students join by code, which produces firstname.lastname.schoolcode. Both formats work for logging in.

{# ── Upload Steps ── #}

Follow these steps to upload your student file.

1. Download and open the template

Download the official template from the Excel Upload section above. Open it in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets. Student data starts at row 5 — rows 1 through 3 contain instructions, and row 4 contains the column headers.

2. Delete the sample rows

The template includes 5 sample student rows. Delete all of them before entering your own data. Do not modify the headers in row 4.

3. Fill in student data

Enter one student per row. Only first_name and last_name are required. Fill in as many optional columns as you can — class group, level, gender, and student ID all help generate richer dashboard analytics. If you want students to log in with a specific password, set their student_id to that password.

4. Save as Excel

Save the file in .xlsx or .xls format. CSV files are not accepted. If you are using Google Sheets, go to File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).

5. Upload on your dashboard

Go to your school dashboard and find the Upload Students section. Click Choose File, select your Excel file, then click Upload. The file must be under 5 MB and contain no more than 1,000 rows.

6. Check the result

You will see a message showing how many accounts were created and how many rows were skipped. Rows are skipped when:

  • first_name or last_name is blank.
  • The student's name (first + last) already exists in your school — duplicates are silently skipped.

After a successful upload, print the claim codes for your students so they can activate their accounts. See Claim Codes for the next steps.

{# ── Join by Code ── #}

If you prefer not to use Excel upload, students can join your school individually using a join code. Each school has a unique 6-character access code (e.g. A3F9B2) and a corresponding QR code.

Generating your access code

On your dashboard, find the access code section in the right sidebar. If you haven't generated a code yet, click Generate Code. This creates a random 6-character code that is unique across all schools on SESA. You can regenerate a new code at any time — the old one stops working immediately.

How students join

  1. Share your access code with the student, or direct them to scan your QR code.
  2. The student goes to the SESA homepage and clicks Join with Code.
  3. They enter the 6-character code. The system confirms your school name.
  4. The student fills in their first name, last name, and chooses a password (minimum 6 characters). They can also add their class group and level.
  5. After submitting, their account is created and they are signed in immediately.

QR code

Your dashboard displays a QR code that encodes your join link. Students can scan it with their phone camera to go directly to the join page, pre-linked to your school. From your dashboard, you can also open a printable QR flyer — print it and display it on your school notice board for easy access.

Username format for self-joined students

When students join by code, their username is generated as {firstname}.{lastname}.{schoolcode} (e.g. kofi.mensah.acraa). This is different from the format used in bulk upload ({first_initial}{lastname}). Both formats work for logging in.

Use bulk upload for onboarding many students at once at the start of term. Use join by code for students who enrol mid-term, or for schools that prefer students to create their own accounts.
{# ── Claim Codes ── #}

When you bulk-upload students, their accounts are created but not yet activated. Each student receives a unique 6-character claim code that they must use to set their own password and activate their account.

Printing claim codes

After a successful upload, click Print Claim Codes on your dashboard. This opens a printable page with individual slips for each unclaimed student. Each slip shows:

  • The student's name and class
  • Their auto-generated username
  • Their 6-character claim code
  • The claim URL (yourdomain.com/claim)

Cut the slips and distribute them to students. Students should keep their claim code private — anyone with the code can activate the account.

How students claim their account

  1. Go to the SESA homepage and click Sign In.
  2. On the login page, click Activate Account (or go directly to /claim).
  3. Enter the 6-character claim code from their slip.
  4. Set a new password (minimum 6 characters) and confirm it.
  5. Click Activate Account. The student is now signed in and can access the platform.

Important details

  • Students cannot log in until they claim their account. If they try to sign in before claiming, they will be redirected to the claim page.
  • Claim codes are case-sensitive — students should enter them exactly as shown on the slip.
  • Once claimed, the claim code is no longer valid. Each code can only be used once.
  • If a student loses their claim code, the school admin can re-print claim codes from the dashboard to get a new copy.

Initial passwords (before claiming)

Before claiming, the student's temporary password is either their student_id from the Excel file (if provided), or their auto-generated username. However, since the account is not claimed yet, they cannot use this password to log in — they must go through the claim flow first.

{# ── Password Reset ── #}

If a student forgets their password, they can reset it themselves — no email required. The reset process uses the school's access code as verification.

How to reset

  1. Go to the SESA login page and click Forgot your password?
  2. Enter the school access code (the same 6-character code used for student join).
  3. Enter the student's username.
  4. Enter and confirm a new password (minimum 8 characters).
  5. Click Reset Password. The student can now log in with the new password.

What the student needs

The student must know two things to reset their password:

  • Their school's access code — the same code used to join the school.
  • Their username — the auto-generated username assigned during upload or self-registration.

If the student does not know their username, the school admin can find it on the dashboard in the student list.

The access code acts as a shared secret. Anyone who knows the school's access code and a student's username can reset that student's password. For this reason, schools should treat the access code as confidential and regenerate it periodically from the dashboard.
{# ── Troubleshooting ── #}

"Upload is not enabled"

Your subscription has not been activated yet. Go to your dashboard and click the Subscribe button to complete payment via Paystack. Once payment is confirmed, upload is enabled instantly.

"Missing required columns: first_name, last_name"

The column headers in your file don't match the template exactly. Make sure the headers in row 4 are spelled first_name and last_name — all lowercase, with underscores, no spaces, no extra characters. Do not modify the template headers.

"Invalid file type"

Only .xlsx and .xls files are accepted. CSV files are not supported. If you are using Google Sheets, go to File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) before uploading.

Some rows were skipped

Rows are skipped when first_name or last_name is blank. Rows are also skipped when the combination of first name and last name already exists in your school — this prevents duplicate accounts. Check your file for empty rows or duplicate names and re-upload.

Level or gender not showing correctly

These fields only accept exact lowercase values. For level: jhs, shs, or university. For gender: male, female, or other. Any other value — including capitalised versions like JHS or Male — will be ignored.

Account locked after too many attempts

After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes and try again. If the lockout keeps happening, the school admin should verify the correct username and password.

Student can't log in — redirected to claim page

The student's account has not been claimed yet. They must go to /claim, enter their claim code, and set a new password before they can log in. See Claim Codes for the full flow.

Claim code not working

Claim codes are case-sensitive — enter them exactly as shown on the slip. Each code can only be used once. If the code still doesn't work, the student may have already claimed the account, or the code may have been regenerated. Re-print claim codes from the dashboard to get updated slips.

Student forgot their password

Students can reset their own password at the login page by clicking Forgot your password?. They need their school's access code and their username. If they don't know their username, you can find it on your dashboard in the student list. See Password Reset for details.

Students can't find the login page

Direct students to the SESA homepage and tell them to click Sign In. They should select the regular login (not School Administrator Login). They log in with their username (not email) and their password.

"Username or password is incorrect"

Students log in with their username, not their email. Usernames are auto-generated — the student cannot choose their own. If a student doesn't know their username, you can find it on your dashboard. If they've forgotten their password, they can reset it using the school access code.
