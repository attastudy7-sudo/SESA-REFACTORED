## Design Context

### Users
**Who they are:** School administrators, guidance counselors, and head teachers at Ghanaian primary/JHS/SHS schools. Non-technical users who care deeply about student welfare but have limited time and resources. Some are government officials at the Ghana Education Service.

**Context:** Consuming this landing page on a desktop during school hours, or on a mobile phone while traveling between schools. They need to understand quickly what SESA offers and feel confident it's legitimate, GES-aligned, and trustworthy.

**Job to be done:** Decide whether to register their school for the SESA assessment program. Must feel trust, clarity, and institutional credibility.

### Brand Personality
**Voice:** Warm & Authoritative — like a trusted school principal speaking gently to a concerned parent. Not coldly clinical, not overly sentimental. Grounded, professional, caring.

**Tone words:** Grounded · Dignified · Gentle · Trustworthy · Ghanaian

**Emotional goal:** The reader should feel: "This is a serious, reputable organization that truly understands our students' needs."

### Aesthetic Direction
**Visual tone:** Calm & Reflective — warm forest greens, aged gold accents, ivory paper-like backgrounds. Editorial elegance with institutional credibility. Think of a well-bound library book in a Ghanaian school office.

**Theme:** Light mode (ivory/forest). This is a daytime professional tool for educators, not a late-night consumer app.

**Motion language:** Gentle fade-ups with slight vertical drift. Content "breathes" into view like pages being turned. No flashy entrances — everything should feel like it was always there, just gently revealed.

**Typography:** Playfair Display (serif, headings) + Manrope (sans-serif, body) — established and not changing.

### Design Principles
1. **Reveal, don't decorate** — Every animation should serve clarity and comprehension, not spectacle. Content reveals in reading order so the eye follows naturally.
2. **Staggered rhythm** — Groups of cards/items reveal one after another with a gentle delay, creating a cadence that feels thoughtful, not rushed.
3. **Respect motion preferences** — All animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No infinite loops or distracting auto-play.
4. **Performance first** — Animate only `opacity` and `transform`. No layout-triggering properties. Use `will-change` sparingly.
5. **Ghanaian warmth** — The gold accents and warm ivory create a sense of sunlight and paper. Animations should feel organic, not mechanical — use ease-out quart/expo, not linear or cubic-bezier tricks.
